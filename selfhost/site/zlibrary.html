To download books here, paste your Z-Library sign-in cookie. ReKindle keeps it with your ReKindle account and only uses it to download the books you choose.

  1. On a computer, sign in at z-lib.sk.
  2. Open the developer tools (right-click, Inspect), then Application (Chrome, Edge) or Storage (Firefox, Safari), Cookies, https://z-lib.sk.
  3. Copy the values of remix_userid and remix_userkey.
  4. Paste them below as: remix_userid=VALUE; remix_userkey=VALUE
